Gem Codex

Discover the powers waiting inside every gem. Select an entry to explore its abilities and passive effects. Details are based on the BlissGems and BlissGems Expansion references.

The original collection

BlissGems

8 gems
Astra Gem

Abilities

  • Astral Daggers: Fire three phantom daggers in a spread, dealing damage at range.
  • Astral Projection (Tier 2): Leave your body in spectator form for five seconds; tag players or spook nearby foes while projected.
  • Dimensional Drift (Tier 2): Ride an invisible mount with a subtle astral trail.
  • Dimensional Void (Tier 2): Suppress nearby enemy gem abilities and weaken them.

Passives Chance to negate incoming damage, heal from kills, and capture/release souls.

Fire Gem

Abilities

  • Charged Fireball: Charge and launch an explosive fireball; damage and blast size scale with charge.
  • Cozy Campfire (Tier 2): A healing fire for allies that burns nearby enemies.
  • Crisp (Tier 2): Evaporate water and convert nearby terrain to Nether blocks.
  • Meteor Shower (Tier 2): Call burning meteors down over a distant target area.

Passives Fire Resistance and automatic ore smelting. Tier 2 adds Flame and Fire Aspect II.

Flux Gem

Abilities

  • Flux Beam: Charge and fire a long-range beam that damages armor; trusted players' armor is repaired instead.
  • Ground (Tier 2): Stun a targeted foe at range.

Passives Cleanse Weakness, Slowness, and Hunger; arrows shock targets. Flow State rewards repeated actions with speed.

Life Gem

Abilities

  • Heart Drainer: Apply Wither II to a targeted entity.
  • Circle of Life (Tier 2): Create an area that regenerates you and withers enemies.

Passives Regeneration, extra damage to undead, increased saturation from food, and stronger golden-apple absorption.

Puff Gem

Abilities

  • Dash: Burst forward in the direction you face.
  • Breezy Bash (Tier 2): Launch upward and gain temporary fall immunity.

Passives No fall damage, a mid-air double jump, and a launch effect on hit. Tier 2 also prevents sculk sensors and shriekers from triggering.

Speed Gem

Abilities

  • Sloth's Sedative: Slow and fatigue nearby enemies.
  • Speed Storm (Tier 2): Gain a powerful burst of Speed.
  • Adrenaline Rush (Tier 2): Enter a short combat state with energy-scaled speed and faster attacks.

Passives Speed and Dolphin's Grace; Tier 2 strengthens the speed effect. Tier 2 adds Efficiency V to tools.

Strength Gem

Abilities

  • Bloodthorns: Damage nearby foes, with greater power at higher health.
  • Frailer Power (Tier 2): Weaken and wither a targeted player.
  • Chad Strength (Tier 2): Gain Strength II and Resistance II temporarily.

Passives Strength and charged critical-hit tracking. Tier 2 adds Sharpness V to swords and axes.

Wealth Gem

Abilities

  • Durability Chip: Toggle double durability damage dealt by your tools.
  • Rich Rush (Tier 2): Gain Haste III and Luck IV for mining and farming.
  • Tier 2 utilities: Open personal pockets, amplify active potion effects, or toggle auto-smelting.

Passives Luck and Hero of the Village; target players can be afflicted with Unluck. Tier 2 adds Fortune III, Looting III, and Mending.

BlissGems Expansion

33 gems
Frost Gem

Abilities

  • Ice Wall (15s): Raise a temporary 5×3 packed-ice wall.
  • Frost Nova (30s): Damage and freeze enemies in a 6-block ring.
  • Blizzard (60s): A snow tornado damages and encases nearby foes.
  • Avalanche (40s): Five shockwaves launch, freeze, and damage enemies.

Passives Chilling Touch frosts water underfoot; Tier 2 grants Fire Resistance.

Terra Gem

Abilities

  • Earthquake (15s): Send erupting terrain forward to launch and slow foes.
  • Stone Skin (30s): Gain Resistance III, with Slowness II.
  • Rock Shield (45s): Orbiting boulders damage and knock back attackers.
  • Meteor (50s): Strike a target area with an igniting magma meteor.

Passives Slow Falling; Tier 2 reveals nearby creature footsteps while grounded.

Aqua Gem

Abilities

  • Tsunami (15s): Push enemies back with a forward-rushing wave.
  • Riptide (8s): Propel yourself forward, damaging what you pass through.
  • Drown (30s): Trap a targeted entity with Slowness, Blindness, and Poison.
  • Whirlpool (45s): Pull nearby entities inward with a vortex.

Passives Water Breathing; Speed and Strength in water or rain; Tier 2 grants Dolphin's Grace.

Toxic Gem

Abilities

  • Acid Geyser (15s): Leave a poisonous, slowing acid pool where a bolt lands.
  • Toxic Tornado (30s): A growing venom tornado inflicts Weakness, Blindness, and Levitation.
  • Vampiric Swarm (20s): Hunting bats damage your target and heal you.
  • Pandemic (45s): Spread Wither, Poison, and Nausea across a wide area.

Passives Poison and Nausea immunity; Tier 2 poisons melee attackers.

Illusion Gem

Abilities

  • Shadow Clones (30s): Exploding clones hunt nearby enemies.
  • Switch (20s): Swap places with a target and leave them disoriented.
  • Dark Room (30s): Blind and slow nearby foes with Darkness.
  • Paranoia (45s): Blind a target amid phantom sounds and souls.

Passives Invisibility; Tier 2 grants Night Vision.

Mecha Gem

Abilities

  • Evoker Fangs (15s): Send rings of fangs erupting outward.
  • Turret (30s): Deploy an arrow-firing turret.
  • EMP (45s): Expand a dome that damages, blinds, and slows.
  • Block Golem (90s): Assemble a powerful combat construct.

Passives Haste II; Tier 2 grants Resistance I.

Chrono Gem

Abilities

  • Rewind (30s): Return along your recorded 5-second trail, restoring the health you had then.
  • Stasis (30s): Freeze your targeted foe in place.
  • Time Dilation (45s): Speed yourself up and slow enemies in a temporal field.
  • Echo (60s): Replay your recent path as an attacking copy.

Passives Haste; Tier 2 grants Regeneration.

Gravity Gem

Abilities

  • Crush (15s): Slam and pin a targeted foe.
  • Levitate (30s): Lift an enemy before dropping them.
  • Singularity (45s): Pull nearby entities inward, then collapse for damage.
  • Inversion (60s): Suspend everyone in a wide dome before gravity returns.

Passives Jump Boost II; Tier 2 slows entities close to you.

Storm Gem

Abilities

  • Chain Lightning (15s): Arc a bolt between up to five foes.
  • Static Field (30s): A charged ring follows you, granting Speed and shocking enemies.
  • Downburst (45s): Blast nearby enemies into the air, then send out a ground shockwave.
  • Thunderdome (60s): A storm strikes random foes inside its dome.

Passives Speed; Tier 2 grants Strength during rain or storms.

Sonic Gem

Abilities

  • Sonic Boom (15s): Blast a cone of piercing damage and knockback.
  • Echolocation (30s): Reveal nearby living things through walls, visible only to you.
  • Deafen (30s): Blind and disorient a targeted foe.
  • Resonance (60s): Deliver four pulses of piercing sonic damage.

Passives Nearby mobs lose interest in you; Tier 2 senses living things through walls.

Wood Gem

Abilities

  • Vine Grapple (10s): Swing to a block or pull a foe toward you.
  • Bark Armor (30s): Gain Absorption and Resistance with orbiting bark plates.
  • Grasping Roots (40s): Root and damage nearby enemies.
  • Seed Bomb (55s): Grow an oak that launches nearby foes.

Passives Regeneration in open daylight; Tier 2 grants Saturation.

Spirit Gem

Abilities

  • Soul Tether (14s): Chain a foe to you and yank them back if they stray.
  • Guardian Orb (30s): A spirit orb intercepts incoming projectiles.
  • Spirit Pack (45s): Summon spirit wolves to hunt nearby foes.
  • Possession (60s): Control a targeted mob temporarily; players cannot be possessed.

Passives See nearby players' health; Tier 2 grants Slow Falling.

Light Gem

Abilities

  • Ricochet Beam (14s): Bounce a damaging beam off walls between foes.
  • Flashbang (28s): Blind foes who can see a thrown flash.
  • Light Bridge (30s): Build a temporary bridge of solid light.
  • Judgment (60s): Strike nearby foes with pillars of light.

Passives Speed in open daylight; Tier 2 resists Blindness and Darkness and reveals invisible foes.

Windy Gem

Abilities

  • Gust (12s): Blast foes and blow away nearby projectiles and items.
  • Wind Wall (28s): Turn incoming projectiles around and push back foes.
  • Tornado (45s): A guided tornado spins up foes and loose items.
  • Gale Force (60s): Launch nearby foes skyward and slam them down.

Passives Speed; Tier 2 prevents fall damage and enables a double jump.

Metal Gem

Abilities

  • Disarm (18s): Knock a metal weapon from a targeted player's hand.
  • Reflective Plating (30s): Gain Resistance and reflect melee damage.
  • Iron Maiden (40s): Trap a target in damaging iron bars.
  • Heat Metal (60s): Burn nearby foes wearing metal armor.

Passives Resistance; Tier 2 grants Strength.

Death Gem Mythic

Abilities

  • Reaper's Scythe (20s): Sweep foes with Wither; low-health targets take extra damage.
  • Death Mark (35s): Mark a foe to make them glow and take increased damage.
  • Raise Dead (50s): Summon skeletons to fight for you.
  • Life Ledger (70s): Return damage taken during the effect to your attackers.

Passives Heal on kills; Tier 2 grants Strength.

Sun Gem Legendary

Abilities

  • Solar Flare (18s): Fire a piercing beam that burns foes and transforms the impact terrain.
  • High Noon (40s): Pull distant foes into blazing sunlight and weaken them.
  • Corona (55s): Surround yourself with a damaging aura and gain Fire Resistance.
  • Supernova (180s): Guide a miniature sun that detonates on impact.

Passives Fire Resistance; Tier 2 stores solar charge for stronger solar abilities.

Diamond Gem

Abilities

  • Crystal Cage (35s): Trap a targeted foe in a breakable cage.
  • Shatter (45s): Explode your standing crystal walls and cages.

Passives Haste; Tier 2 reveals nearby ores through walls.

  • Mirror Clone (50s): Create a mirrored copy that retaliates when struck.
